Creative vision, research context, and project aims
Mood Garden is the name I've given to my music program. I like piano music, melodies and film scores so that's what I'm aiming to do with it essentially. The idea is to create some relaxing music in a variety of moods. You'd be able to pick a mood (e.g. "Rainy Evening", "Midnight Dream") and then tweak how the music sounds. I'd also like to have a garden interface where the flowers represent different sounds. I'm still getting to grips with SuperCollider so I'll start with the fundamentals of music and controls before attempting anything more complex.

My main aims are:
•	To create music using chords, simple melodies, and different mood presets.
•	To make a simple interface that lets users’ control and change the music.
•	To explore how visuals, such as flowers and a garden, could make the process of creating music more interesting.
•	I'll look at some examples of interactive musical instruments and consider how we can make the controls easy to understand.

Project research and AI collaboration strategy
I'm going to look at interactive music projects and see how SuperCollider can be used to generate sounds and melodies. I'll also look at p5.js as a potential option for the visual interface.
My plan is to use ChatGPT to help me understand the code, debug it and work through various ideas. I may also use GitHub Copilot. I'll be looking at the code myself and checking the suggestions against the documentation to get a feel for how the program actually works. I don't want to just go with the AI's suggestions and make all my own decisions about the sounds and the design.

Project development plan
I'll start off with a basic music generator, then add in the chords, the melodies and a few presets. Then I'll move on to the interface. I'll get the key bits working first and only add on to that if I've got sufficient time.

What do you expect users to hear?
Users will hear relaxing music with piano-like sounds, chords and soft background sounds. Each preset will have a different mood and the melody will vary but will stay within the appropriate set of notes.

How will users interact with your software?
Users will be able to start and stop the music, pick a mood and tweak a few of the settings (e.g. brightness, how often the notes play). I'm going to use either SuperCollider or p5.js for the interface, whichever is best for the project. If I have time I'd like to add in a garden where you click on the flowers and the sounds change.

What unique features does your software offer them?
The concept is really to enable people to make music without having to play an instrument or have any real understanding of the various musical controls. The mood presets will give you a point of departure and the settings allow you to play around. The garden concept could also be a way of making the experience more visual.
How will you approach AI collaboration during development?

I'll use AI when I'm stuck on some code or need help with a technical concept. I'll assess the suggestions and make the necessary amendments myself. I'll keep a record of the key AI conversations and ensure I can explain the code in the viva.
